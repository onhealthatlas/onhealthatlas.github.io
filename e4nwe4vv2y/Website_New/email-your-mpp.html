Tell your Member of Provincial Parliament what you think about access to family doctors. Find your MPP with your postal code, check the message, and send it from your own email. It also goes to the Premier and the Minister of Health.

Using Gmail, Outlook.com or another email website? Start a new email, then copy the addresses above, the subject and the message into it.

Nothing you type here is sent to us or saved. The message goes from your own email account, and only when you send it.

Tips for a message that gets read

  • Include your postal code. MPPs give priority to the people they represent.
  • Add a sentence or two about your own experience, if you’re comfortable sharing it.
  • Be polite and specific, and ask a question you’d like answered.
  • Keep a copy. If you don’t hear back in a few weeks, follow up.

Where the numbers come from

The suggested message uses the Ontario total, and the share of family doctors in your riding who are 60 or older, from the map The family doctor shortage, 2026. Riding figures are estimates built from municipal figures in the Ontario Rostering Physician and Patient Counts Report, January 2026.

Your riding is found from the first three characters of your postal code, using where people lived in the 2021 Census. Where those three characters cover more than one riding, you choose yours.
